Privacy Policy - Row & Stitch

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Row & Stitch ("the app") is developed by Upboing. The app works offline and stores your projects, counters and settings only on your device.

Project data

Your projects, counters, notes and settings are stored on your device. There is no app account or sign-up. We do not receive the content of your projects.

Advertising and data used by Google

The free version includes Google AdMob banner and rewarded ads. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, other app interactions, crash logs and performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, diagnose problems, and improve its advertising services. This information may be shared with Google and its advertising partners. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's iOS data disclosure.

This app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission to access Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). That does not mean the ad SDK processes no data or uses no other identifiers. Ad availability and personalization may vary by region and device settings. Paid Pro users do not see ads in the app.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Backups

Project backups are exported by you, to a destination you choose. We never receive them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@upboing.com.
