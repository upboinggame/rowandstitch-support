Privacy Policy - Row & Stitch

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Row & Stitch ("the app") is developed by Upboing. The app works offline and stores your projects, counters and settings only on your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, and no tracking.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve ads according to Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The Pro version contains no ads.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Backups

Project backups are exported by you, to a destination you choose. We never receive them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@upboing.com.
